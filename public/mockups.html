Creator flow

  1. Attach TikTok, Reels or Shorts account once.
  2. Pick an offer and read rules.
  3. System finds suitable videos or accepts direct submission.
  4. AI checks language, dates, brand safety and duplicates.
  5. Advertiser approves report, then USDT payout opens.

Advertiser cabinet

Advertiser controls budget, approvals and growth signal.

Advertiser flow

  1. Deposit USDT to company balance.
  2. Create offer: rate per 1k views, languages, dates and rights.
  3. Set stop tracking by reserved budget and report date.
  4. Approve creators, videos and final accepted views.
  5. Review top videos, comments, creators and API conversions.
